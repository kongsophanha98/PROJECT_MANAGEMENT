# Stakeholders: first list

The two stakeholders with high influence and high interest are S01 (the Head of the IT department, who funds the project and decides at the week-7 and week-13 gates) and S02 (the supervising lecturer, who manages the plan and approves changes within the budget). They get the PM's attention first because every scope, schedule and budget decision passes through them, and a missing decision from either one stops the team.

S09 (the data protection officer) is the veto stakeholder: the pilot with 50 students cannot run without approval for storing student names and results, so the data description must reach them well before week 13.

Lab-11 will refine this list into a stakeholder register and an engagement plan.
